DATA ANALYST
Tidewell Analytics, Belgrade (hybrid)

About us
Tidewell Analytics helps retail chains understand their customers. Our analysts turn
sales and loyalty data into decisions about pricing, stock and promotions for clients
in six countries.

What you will do
- Answer business questions with data and present the answers to client teams
- Build and maintain dashboards that managers open every morning
- Clean, join and check data from several sources before it is analysed

What we require
- Strong knowledge of SQL
- At least 3 years of experience writing SQL queries on large datasets
- Excel and Python for data cleaning and analysis
- Experience building dashboards in Power BI, Tableau or a similar tool
- Solid understanding of statistics (hypothesis testing, regression)
- Ability to present findings to non-technical stakeholders
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field (mathematics, statistics, economics or
  computer science)
- English, written and spoken, at B2 level or higher

Nice to have
- Experience with a cloud data warehouse such as BigQuery or Snowflake
- Experience designing and analysing A/B tests

We offer
- A salary of 2,500 to 3,800 EUR net, depending on experience
- 25 days of annual leave and two remote days a week
- A yearly budget for courses and conferences

How to apply
Send your CV to careers@tidewell.example. We reply within two weeks.
