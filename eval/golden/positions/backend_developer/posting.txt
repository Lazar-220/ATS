BACKEND DEVELOPER
Brightway Systems, Belgrade (hybrid)

About us
Brightway Systems builds route-planning software for regional logistics companies.
Our platform processes about two million deliveries a month, and the backend team of
eight engineers keeps it fast and reliable.

What you will do
- Design, build and maintain the services behind our web and mobile apps
- Improve the performance of database queries and background jobs
- Review code, write tests and join the on-call rotation once every two months

What we require
- Strong knowledge of Python
- At least 3 years of professional experience with Python
- Extensive experience designing and building REST APIs
- Working knowledge of SQL, ideally with PostgreSQL
- Experience writing automated tests with pytest or a similar framework
- Git and Docker in daily work
- Bachelor's degree in computer science or a related field
- English, written and spoken, at B2 level or higher

Nice to have
- Experience with message queues such as RabbitMQ or Kafka
- Experience with a cloud provider (AWS, GCP or Azure)

We offer
- A salary of 3,000 to 4,500 EUR net, depending on experience
- 25 days of annual leave and a flexible start to the working day
- A yearly budget for courses and conferences

How to apply
Send your CV to jobs@brightway.example. We reply within two weeks.
