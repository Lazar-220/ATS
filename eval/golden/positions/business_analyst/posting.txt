BUSINESS ANALYST
Harbor Insurance Tech, Belgrade (hybrid)

About us
Harbor Insurance Tech builds the policy and claims systems used by insurers in the
Western Balkans. Our product teams pair business analysts with developers from the
first workshop to the release.

What you will do
- Run workshops with insurance experts and turn what you learn into clear specifications
- Write user stories and keep the backlog ready for each sprint
- Check that what the developers build matches what the business asked for

What we require
- Extensive experience gathering and documenting business requirements
- At least 4 years of experience as a business analyst
- Experience writing user stories and acceptance criteria
- Knowledge of Jira
- At least 2 years of daily work with Jira
- Experience modelling processes in BPMN or UML
- Working knowledge of SQL for ad-hoc data checks
- Bachelor's degree in economics, information systems or a related field
- English, written and spoken, at B2 level or higher

Nice to have
- Experience in insurance or banking
- CBAP or a similar business analysis certification

We offer
- A salary of 2,800 to 4,000 EUR net, depending on experience
- 25 days of annual leave and a flexible working day
- Paid certification exams and a yearly learning budget

How to apply
Send your CV to hiring@harborit.example. We reply within two weeks.
